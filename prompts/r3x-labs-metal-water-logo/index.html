PROMPT

Create a premium square 3D typographic logo reading exactly “R3X LABS”. Use custom aggressive angular, shark-fin-inspired letterforms, dark gunmetal and chrome surfaces, razor-sharp beveled edges, deep extrusion, electric cyan-blue rim lights and reflective wet-metal highlights.

Wrap the full wordmark in a circular explosive water vortex: curling waves, suspended droplets, splash crowns, micro mist and transparent water sheets crossing behind and partially in front of the letters.

Use a pure black cinematic background, centered composition, high contrast, dramatic studio lighting, ultra-detailed PBR metal and physically believable liquid simulation, crisp reflections and premium sports/esports identity styling.

TEXT REQUIREMENT: the only main text must read exactly “R3X LABS”. Keep every character clearly readable. No extra words, no misspellings, no substituted letters.

Why it works

The prompt separates typography, metal treatment, liquid motion and lighting into explicit constraints. The exact-text instruction keeps the visual experiment focused on a usable R3X Labs wordmark rather than decorative pseudo-lettering.

Best use cases

Logo experiments, esports-style identities, music graphics, tech branding, poster titles, social headers and high-energy campaign visuals.
