PROMPT

Create a premium dimensional script logo reading exactly “R3X LABS”. Arrange “R3X” above “LABS” as a tightly interlocked hand-lettered composition with bold retro calligraphic strokes, exaggerated swashes, smooth curves and sharp ornamental terminals.

Make the main letter faces glossy warm ivory and cream enamel with rounded inflated volume and strong specular highlights. Add a thick deep-purple outline and extruded backing layer following every curve to create a raised custom-sign effect.

Place the logo on a pale warm wood-panel background with soft sunlight streaks and gentle realistic cast shadows. Use a high-end 3D sign render, polished candy-like surface and clean premium composition.

TEXT REQUIREMENT: the logo must read exactly “R3X LABS”. No other words, no spelling changes, no random characters.

Why it works

The prompt treats lettering, face material, outline and backing as separate physical layers. That creates the dimensional sign-making look and prevents the purple from simply becoming a flat stroke.

Best use cases

Retro branding, apparel logos, sticker concepts, music graphics, shop signage, social media branding and polished typographic marks.
