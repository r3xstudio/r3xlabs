PROMPT

Create the exact text “R3X LABS” as huge bold industrial block-style 3D typography, arranged “R3X” above “LABS”.

Construct the letters from heavy weathered steel plates with thick beveled frame edges and deep extrusion. Surface treatment: dark oxidized steel, peeling gray paint, layered orange-brown rust, exposed raw metal, corrosion pits, scratches, dents and chipped edges.

Across the lower portion of each letter add worn black-and-yellow diagonal industrial hazard stripes, faded and scratched into the surface. Tiny distressed factory markings may appear only as subtle texture accents and must never compete with the main wording.

Use a dark charcoal seamless studio background, soft rim light, dramatic shadows, hyper-realistic PBR materials and a centered premium product render.

TEXT REQUIREMENT: the only dominant wording must read exactly “R3X LABS”. Keep all letters fully readable. No additional headline text.

Why it works

The effect depends on layered aging: raw steel, oxidized paint, rust, edge damage and hazard markings. Defining each layer separately produces richer corrosion than a generic 'rusty metal' instruction.

Best use cases

Industrial branding, metal and rock artwork, gaming identities, dystopian posters, workshop graphics and rugged product campaigns.
