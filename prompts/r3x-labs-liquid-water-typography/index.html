R3X Labs Liquid Water Typography

Transparent aqua lettering sculpted from liquid water with frozen splash detail on a clean white background.

PROMPT

Create the exact text “R3X LABS” as sculptural 3D lettering made entirely from clear cyan-blue liquid water. Arrange “R3X” above “LABS” in a compact centered composition.

The letterforms must be thick, rounded and fully readable, with transparent glass-like water bodies, internal refraction, caustic highlights, smooth liquid curvature and deeper aqua edges. Add frozen splash crowns, arcs, droplets and fine suspended beads erupting naturally from letter edges without obscuring readability.

Use a clean seamless white-to-pale-gray studio background, subtle soft contact shadow, bright high-key product lighting and crisp photoreal fluid simulation.

TEXT REQUIREMENT: the composition must clearly read exactly “R3X LABS”. No extra text, no added symbols, no misspellings.

Why it works

The prompt specifies both the optical properties of water and the physical behavior of splashes. Keeping the background minimal makes the material effect easy to read and isolates the typography.

Best use cases

Clean tech branding, beverage concepts, summer campaigns, splash graphics, product ads, editorial typography and material studies.
