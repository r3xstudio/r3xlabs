R3X Labs Autumn Embroidered Patch

Warm chenille lettering framed by embroidered leaves, acorns and berries in an autumn patch composition.

PROMPT

Create a large premium embroidered chenille patch reading exactly “R3X LABS”, arranged “R3X” above “LABS”.

Make the lettering extremely thick, rounded and fluffy with plush looped chenille texture and raised stitched borders. Use a warm autumn palette: burnt orange, deep cherry red, golden mustard and cream.

Surround the text with a dense handcrafted embroidered composition of autumn maple and oak leaves, acorns, red berries, small cream cloud-like tufts and tiny golden star stitches. Add a dark burnt-orange embroidered outer border, visible thread direction, tactile raised layers, realistic fabric fibers and stitching.

Isolate the patch on a clean white background with a soft studio shadow and premium collectible patch presentation.

TEXT REQUIREMENT: the central lettering must read exactly “R3X LABS”. No other words or lettering.

Why it works

The prompt defines a clear foreground hierarchy: large chenille text first, seasonal embroidery second. Restricting decoration to recognizable autumn objects prevents the border from becoming visual noise.

Best use cases

Seasonal merchandise, fall campaigns, embroidered patch concepts, cozy apparel graphics, craft branding and autumn social assets.
