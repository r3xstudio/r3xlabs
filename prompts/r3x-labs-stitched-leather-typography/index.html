PROMPT

Create the exact text “R3X LABS” as bold uppercase collegiate block letters arranged “R3X” above “LABS”.

Construct every character from premium handcrafted dark cognac-brown leather with authentic natural grain, pores and subtle tonal variation. Give the letters thick padded embossed volume, softly rounded beveled edges and raised smooth tan leather piping around the perimeter.

Add precise cream saddle stitching around the inner perimeter of each letter, with realistic thread tension, individual stitch detail and artisan construction. Use subtle highlights and soft cast shadows, centered isolated composition on a clean white studio background, photorealistic material rendering and crisp high-end product photography.

TEXT REQUIREMENT: show only the exact readable text “R3X LABS”. No extra lettering or logos.

Why it works

The prompt combines leather grain, padding, piping and stitching as independent construction cues. That makes the result feel manufactured and tactile rather than like a simple leather texture mapped onto text.

Best use cases

Fashion branding, western and heritage graphics, premium monograms, patches, apparel artwork, product labels and handcrafted identity concepts.
