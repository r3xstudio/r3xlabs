PROMPT

Create the exact text “R3X LABS” as large freestanding block letters constructed entirely from smooth naturally rounded river stones and weathered pebbles. Arrange “R3X” on the top row and “LABS” on the bottom.

Each letter must be structurally clear and readable, built from individually shaped stones in ivory, warm beige, pale gray, limestone and soft taupe. Show natural pores, tiny cracks, mineral veins, worn edges and realistic weight. Stones should appear carefully stacked with believable balance and small gaps, forming clean counters and negative spaces.

Use a seamless pale-gray studio background and matte-white surface, soft diffused lighting, gentle contact shadows, minimalist luxury product photography and tactile photorealism.

TEXT REQUIREMENT: the structure must clearly read exactly “R3X LABS”. No extra text, no random characters.

Why it works

The prompt turns each glyph into an architectural stack instead of simply applying a stone texture. Color range, stone scale, negative space and gravity cues help preserve believable construction.

Best use cases

Nature branding, wellness graphics, architectural typography, eco campaigns, tactile lettering studies and minimalist product-style artwork.
