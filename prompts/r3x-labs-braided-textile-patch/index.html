PROMPT

Create a premium embroidered textile badge reading exactly “R3X LABS”, arranged “R3X” above “LABS”.

Build every letter from thick braided cord and yarn with visibly interwoven fibers and plush padded volume. Use a pastel palette of candy pink, mint green, soft lavender and cream, alternating colors by character. Add raised cream rope outlines around each letter and a lavender stitched outer patch border.

Use rounded bubble-like letter shapes, realistic textile weave, thread fuzz, tiny fiber detail, handcrafted dimensional embroidery and clean edge stitching. Center the patch on a pure white background with a subtle soft studio shadow.

TEXT REQUIREMENT: the badge must clearly read exactly “R3X LABS”. No extra words or decorative text.

Why it works

Material construction, color assignment and border hierarchy are separated clearly. This helps the model create actual braided cords and embroidered depth rather than a flat knitted texture.

Best use cases

Craft branding, playful merchandise, embroidered badge concepts, kids apparel, pastel identity systems and tactile social graphics.
